Set up on iPad

  1. Safari on iPad can't talk to MIDI keyboards. Install the free Web MIDI Browser app from the App Store.
  2. Connect the LUMI to the iPad with a USB‑C cable (or a Lightning to USB 3 Camera Adapter on older iPads). Bluetooth works too once it's paired, see the note below.
  3. Open this page's link in Web MIDI Browser and tap Start. The status at the top turns green when the LUMI is found.
  4. Pick a song, keep Learn selected and press the keys that light up. The song waits for you.

Modes

Learn lights the next notes and waits until you play them. Play along keeps time and lights each note just before it's due. Listen plays the song and lights the keys so you can watch.

Your own songs

Tap Open MIDI… to load any .mid file from the Files app. With two tracks, the higher one becomes the right hand. With one track, notes from middle C up are the right hand.

Bluetooth

iPadOS only pairs Bluetooth MIDI from inside a music app. Pair the LUMI once in an app such as GarageBand (Settings › Advanced › Bluetooth MIDI Devices), then come back here. A cable is simpler and has no lag.

If the keys don't light

  • Check that "Send lights to" on the MIDI tab names the LUMI.
  • Set the keyboard octave here to match the LUMI's octave buttons, or turn on "Fit octave to each song".
  • Notes outside the LUMI's keys still show on screen. They just can't light.
  • If an old app put a custom program on the LUMI, a factory reset in ROLI Dashboard brings back the light-up behaviour.

Works with ROLI LUMI Keys (also sold as ROLI Piano M). Any other MIDI keyboard can play the sounds and songs. Only LUMI keys light up.

Light Keys

Plays music from your ROLI LUMI and lights up its keys, without the ROLI app.
